# Intro to Sewing Machines 
A  a fun and simple beginner’s guide to understanding and using sewing machines.

---

### What are you going to learn?

To introduce the sewing machine in a fun and simple way, helping beginners understand how it works, how to use it safely, and how to get started with basic sewing techniques. 

---

### What?

**Didactics**  
- **Competence**: Sewing machine operation
- **Theme**: Sewing
- **Learning objective**: 
    - Learn sewing machine parts and how they work
    - Learn how to thread and set up the machine
    - Practice sewing on paper
    - Try sewing two fabrics together

---

### Who, where, how long

- **Number of students**: between 5 and 30 students  
- **Location**: anywhere with electricity
- **Time**: 75 minutes  
  *(10 – 15 minutes per step)*

---

### Prepare and bring

- what to bring
    - sewing machines, thread, paperprintouts, fabric, pins, and tools
