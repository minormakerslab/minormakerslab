## The Makerslab
We mainly work at [Makerslab of the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences](https://www.amsterdamuas.com/research/labs/makerslab) 

In the Makerslab you can make anything yourself, we won't make it for you. There are always experts around to answer your questions or to help you use a machine or technique safely. There is no charge for use of the machines. Some materials are available for sale in the lab, other materials you will have to bring yourself.

We have a large variety of digital production tools. Such as: Laser cutters, 3D printers, sticker printers, vacuum formers, a UV printer, a RISO printer & many more. There is also a workspace available, with various hand tools. 

![selection of machines](/assets/images/makerslab_machines.jpg)

For more information about the AUAS Makerslab, their machines, materials and methods have a look on their website: [AUAS Makerslab](https://www.amsterdamuas.com/research/labs/makerslab)

## The Bio Lab

We also make use of the bio lab at the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences. We use this lab to get more knowledge about biomaterials. We experiment with different materials and techniques. We use this lab in our weekly assingments and for students who want to explore more they will use the biolab in their project work.
